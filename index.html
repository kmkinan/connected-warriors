What We Do

Our Programs

Trauma-Conscious Yoga

Evidence-based yoga classes specifically designed for trauma survivors, offered at VA Medical Centers and community spaces.

  • Clinically informed practices
  • Safe, trauma-conscious environment
  • Accessible to all levels
  • Always free

Teacher Training

Comprehensive certification programs for yoga teachers who want to serve the military and first responders.

  • Yoga Alliance-aligned training
  • Military cultural competency
  • Trauma-conscious methodology
  • Ongoing support for graduates
